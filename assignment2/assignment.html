Building block

Why lego?

Lego has the inherent benefit of being associated with playfulness by being a toy. However the creative freedom, accesibility and ease of use make it a great fit iteration of protoypes. In other words, lego is a great tinkering material.

For people unfamiliar with modern lego building techniques, there are certain blocks called 'SNOT' blocks nowadays. This acronym stands for Studs Not On Top. The current SNOT bricks that lego makes, have additional studs on the side(s). There is currently no lego brick that has studs on the top and bottom, though there are plenty of building techniques to reverse the stud direction by using a number of other bricks. My first concept, was to make a block that can do this by itsself.

For my second concept, I was reminded of making suspension for lego vehicles. The current lego springs are based around lego technic rather than the basic lego connections. So, why not create a brick that does exactly that? Or even a brick that combines both concepts?
