About me

My name is Joran Faber. I have completed my bachelor's degree in computing science at the Rijksuniversiteit Groningen and I am currently working on a MSc in robotics at the University of Twente.

tinkering & me
For as long as I can remember, I enjoyed disassembling and reassembling objects. Whether they were lego sets, toys with interesting mechanics or my first electronics. This got out of hand in my secondary education, where we had research and development as a subject. It allowed me to use more materials and tools, with teachers to guide the process and help us develop different skills.
Nowadays, I am fortunate enough to have access to a shed where I can use (power)tools and have plenty of space to work on projects. This lets me avoid cleaning up the workbench, a great benefit. As for the more technological side of tinkering, I have been working with arduino's and electronical components for years. Whether making and controlling small robots, reading out sensors or hacking together effect pedals for electronic guitars that can pull double duty as tazers.
On the other hand, I also get to do some more low tech tinkering. I work at a history museum, where I get to use a small blacksmith's workshop. The ability to forge and heat treat metal has come in useful plenty of times during tinkering.
